## 1. Design Language Overview

Contra Labs pairs **classical editorial serif gravitas** (`Source Serif 4 Variable`) with **utilitarian neo-grotesque clarity** (`GT Standard`), set on warm archival paper tones (`#fbfaf6`) and sage-mint washes (`#c8e1dd`). Its visual signature is a tension between **Renaissance/Neoclassical oil-painting portraiture** and **high-contrast halftone dot-matrix overlays** (representing laptops, styluses, and evaluation interfaces), unified by a subtle global film-grain texture.
